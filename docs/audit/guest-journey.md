# Guest journey audit: West Coast Hosting Co public site

Audited 2026-10-01 against the production build at http://localhost:3124 (sample data). Screenshots in `/tmp/claude-0/audit-guest/*.png`; file references are to `src/**` at the time of audit. Scope: home, /properties, /properties/[slug], /about, /services, /contact, /book/success, /book/cancelled, 404, plus the `/api/checkout` POST. Checked at 390px and 1440px. Contrast figures are WCAG 2.x relative-luminance calculations on the tokens in `src/app/globals.css`.

## Verdict

The site has the right bones for a direct-booking brand: the voice is genuinely warm and personal ("Two friends, real homes, no faceless platform", "You'll hear from Christi or Melissa before you arrive"), the three-home catalogue is easy to scan, and the booking panel asks for very little. But a guest cannot currently complete the journey on a phone: the four primary nav links are hidden below 640px with no menu, the booking panel sits roughly 2,000px down the detail page with no sticky call to action, and any booking mistake (past date, one-night stay, dates taken, or Stripe not configured) dumps the guest onto a raw JSON page because the panel is a native form posting to an API route. On top of that, the brand's Soft Sky Blue (#8fc0ce) is used for every form label and section eyebrow at 1.9:1 contrast, and every primary button is white on Deep Sky Teal at 2.6:1, so the most important text on the site fails WCAG AA. The success and cancelled pages reference an undefined `bg-brand` colour, which makes their only link invisible. Fix the four P0 items and the site becomes bookable and lawful; the P1 list is mostly conversion hygiene (show the total, connect the calendar to the form, hide dev placeholders, give the guest policy information before they pay).

## What already works well (do not change)

- Brand voice is consistently warm and first-person: "We answer our own phones", "Each home is one we know personally, cared for like our own", "ask us; we sometimes know of a gap", "You'll hear from Christi or Melissa before you arrive". Keep this register everywhere, including new error copy.
- Search bar is the right shape (where / dates / guests / search) and stacks cleanly at 390px with no horizontal scroll on any page audited.
- Pricing is honest on the card and panel: nightly rate, cleaning fee and "lodging tax at checkout" are stated before the guest clicks. G6 is about adding the total, not hiding anything.
- Checkout logic is sound: a database exclusion constraint prevents double-booking, a 30-minute Stripe hold with a sweep cron releases abandoned dates, and the three line items (nightly, cleaning, tax) are itemised on Stripe.
- Every `<input>` is wrapped in a `<label>` with visible text, the honeypot field is `aria-hidden` and `tabIndex=-1`, and all phone/email contacts use `tel:`/`mailto:` links.
- Images use `next/image` with `sizes`, the hero is `priority`, and the SVG map art has a proper `role="img"` and descriptive `aria-label`.
- Heading order on home, /properties, /properties/[slug], /about and /contact is correct (single h1, h2 sections, h3 within).
- The "Coast to Cascades" map illustration and the three-pillar "How we host" block are a strong, ownable trust section; the About page copy is personal and specific.
- The contact form's success state ("Got it. We'll be in touch soon, usually the same day.") and the empty-results copy on /properties are exactly the tone the error states in G1, G12 and G25 should copy.
