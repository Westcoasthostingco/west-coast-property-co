# Performance, technical SEO and content audit

West Coast Hosting Co public site, audited 2026-10-01 against the production build running at `http://localhost:3124` (sample data, Clerk pointed at the `example.clerk.accounts.dev` placeholder, `PREVIEW_ROLE` apparently set). Read-only audit; no code changed.

**Scope note.** Another workstream is adding SEO metadata, JSON-LD, `robots.ts`, `sitemap.ts`, an OG image, `llms.txt` and the `/legal/*` pages right now. Those files exist in the working tree (`src/lib/seo.ts`, `src/components/seo/JsonLd.tsx`, `src/app/opengraph-image.tsx`, `src/app/robots.ts`, `src/app/sitemap.ts`, `src/app/llms.txt/route.ts`, `src/app/legal/privacy/page.tsx`) but are newer than the running build (`BUILD_ID` 16:12 UTC), so on the server `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/llms.txt` and `/legal/*` all return 404 and no page carries canonical, Open Graph, Twitter or JSON-LD tags. Their absence is **not** counted as a finding below; the "In progress, re-check after rebuild" section lists what to verify once that work lands.

**Local numbers are only indicative.** Everything was measured on localhost with no network latency, no CDN, Chromium via Playwright with Clerk's third-party scripts blocked (they would hang on the placeholder domain). Treat byte counts as reliable and timings as a floor; real-world LCP will be dominated by the 1 MB page weight and the hero image, not by server time.

## Verdict

The site is in good shape editorially and structurally: the brand name is consistent everywhere (no "Property Co" leftovers in `src`, `docs` or `README`), the tagline, phone numbers, email and locations agree across pages, the tone is warm and specific, every internal link on the public pages resolves, headings are semantic, fonts are self-hosted and subset, images go through `next/image`, and there is zero layout shift. The problems are concentrated in four places: (1) every page is server-rendered on each request because the root layout awaits Clerk's `auth()` and wraps the marketing site in `ClerkProvider`, which also ships ~235 KB gzip of first-party JS plus Clerk's CDN bundles to visitors who only want to look at three houses; (2) the hero image uses a `sizes` hint written for a half-width card, so at 1440 px the browser picks the 750 px candidate and upscales it 2x, and the source photos themselves are only 1200 px wide; (3) a handful of visible placeholders and broken styling slipped through (the "Map of Gig Harbor (Mapbox, step 3)" box on every listing, `bg-brand` buttons that render white-on-cream on the booking result pages, developer jargon on the sign-in fallback, Next's default 404); (4) page titles and descriptions are generic or duplicated outside the property pages. None of these block launch on their own, but P1 through P4 and T1 through T3 should be fixed before the site is announced.
