# Portal audit: owner, admin, cleaner

Audited 2026-10-01 against the production build at http://localhost:3124 (sample data, `PREVIEW_ROLE=admin`). Screenshots were taken at 1440px and 390px with Playwright; behaviour was confirmed against `src/app/{owner,admin,clean}`, `src/components/{owner,admin,clean,charts}` and `src/lib/{owner,admin,metrics,mock,cleaning,cleaner-actions}.ts`. Screenshot names below refer to `/tmp/claude-0/audit-portals/<name>.png`.

Severity: **P0** wrong data or a blocked task · **P1** hurts daily use · **P2** polish.

## Verdicts

**Owner portal.** Dana can answer "how is my home doing" in one glance: four headline tiles, two trend charts, upcoming stays, and a "Scheduled payouts" card are the right things in the right order, and the statement pages are genuinely good (clear fee maths, print button, honest footnote about cleaning fees and invoices). She cannot yet answer "when do I get paid" reliably, because the $12,367 "Scheduled payouts" figure sums every future transfer through December without saying so, the "this month" tiles show a forecast of a month that is one day old and label it as a 39% decline, and today's in-house stays are listed under "Upcoming stays" with a COMPLETED chip. On a phone the statements table hides the only column that matters (Net to you) behind an unsignposted horizontal scroll. Fix the date/status logic and the two labels and this portal is close to shippable.

**Admin back office.** The information architecture is right (Overview, Calendar, Bookings, Cleaning, Payouts, Accounting) and the cleaning board is the strongest page in the app: grouped by day, inline assign and status selects that auto-submit, "Same day" turnover warnings, and a link to the stay. But the admin cannot run a turnover day from the Overview today: the "Turnovers this week" tile says 1 when the board shows 4, because stays that checked in today are marked `completed` by the sample generator and the dashboard filters `completed` out. The master calendar only shows days 1–20 of the month at 1440px and needs a horizontal scroll nobody is told about, and on a phone every admin page renders ~1000px wide because the nav's `shrink-0` items force the grid track open. Payouts is a 150-row unpaginated table sorted by home rather than date, and the Owners page says Marcus has no Stripe account while Payouts says he has been paid $24k. Destructive actions are confirmed, sample mode is honest after the fact but silent before it, and the refund button is offered on bookings that have nothing to refund.

**Cleaner portal.** This is the best-fitted of the three for its task. Jordan in a driveway gets: today's job on top, a 56px "Start job"/"Mark done" bar pinned to the bottom, 56px checklist rows, a door code that only appears on the day and only on tap, photos with camera capture, notes, and a one-tap issue report. Dates are computed in Pacific time (the other two portals use UTC). The remaining problems are small: a duplicated date in the job header ("Thu, Oct 8 · Thu, Oct 8, 2026"), two date formats on the calendar page, truncated "The …" labels in calendar cells, and the same low-contrast caps labels used everywhere else. The one task gap: an unassigned job at a home where Jordan is the default cleaner is invisible to him, so there is no way for a cleaner to pick up open work.

## What already works well

- **Statements** (`/owner/statements/[month]`): one line per stay with gross, fee, net and transfer status, a total row, a print button, and a plain-language footnote that cleaning fees and lodging tax are not owner revenue. This is the model the rest of the portal should follow.
- **Shared metrics** (`src/lib/metrics.ts`): owner and admin compute the same prorated-by-night numbers from one function, so Dana's $5,450 and the admin's $7,130 reconcile across the three homes.
- **Cleaning board**: grouped by day, inline cleaner and status selects that auto-submit with a `<noscript>` fallback, orange "Same day" warnings, a link to the stay, and a past-30-days view with per-cleaner cost. The admin can run a day from it once the overview counts are fixed.
- **Cleaner job page**: 56px tap targets, sticky primary action, optimistic checklist with rollback on failure, door code gated to the day of the clean and fetched on tap (never rendered server-side), camera capture for photos, one-tap issue report that creates a maintenance ticket, Pacific-time date logic with the reasoning documented in `dates.ts`.
- **Destructive actions**: cancel, refund, run payouts and mark-done-with-items-remaining all go through `window.confirm` with a sentence that says what will happen ("Scheduled payouts are held and the turnover is skipped").
- **Honest placeholders**: "Sync coming after launch", "Sample mode: uploads are disabled", "Paying online is coming soon", and the integrations page that reads env vars and never shows a key.
- **Server-action error path**: every admin write returns a sentence, is written to `?notice=`, and renders in a `role="status"` box; sample mode says "nothing was saved" rather than pretending.
- **Charts**: single teal series, no legend noise, a `<figcaption>` with every value for screen readers, and small multiples per home rather than a multi-line tangle.
- **Brand voice**: "Welcome back, Dana", "Hi, Jordan. Nothing on the board today.", "Enjoy the breather", "Text Christi or Melissa" — warm, specific and consistent with the public site.
